## Product and physical scene
Four to ten friends pass a phone around a real table in a noisy room. One shared word drives clues and hidden votes. The interface must make who acts next obvious while keeping private information covered. Wood and lamplight establish the tavern; normal buttons and navigation keep the game usable.

## Variations
1. **Around One Table:** overhead rectangular oak table; seats along the long sides. Clearest spatial roster, familiar reading order. Risk: can feel diagrammatic.
2. **By Lantern Light:** shallow angled table with warm teak surfaces and flat controls. More atmosphere. Risk: perspective competes for scarce phone height.
3. **The Joiner’s Table:** flush walnut table with inlaid edge and tighter working surface. Keeps the private card central and lowers the surrounding scene. Risk: quieter sense of a room.
